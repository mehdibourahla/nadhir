## App Review notes (App Store)

Nadhir works without an account: the map, forecasts, Survival mode and commune alerts need no sign-in. Accounts (Sign in with Apple, Google or email) add watch zones and citizen reports; reviewers can create one in the app.

Background location is opt-in: the "Also follow where I am" switch in the alerts sheet (bell button on the map). The phone resolves its commune from its position against commune outlines shipped in the app, and subscribes to that commune's push topic. Significant-change monitoring keeps it current. Following never sends the exact position; reports and report confirmations do send one, as the privacy policy explains.

Time-sensitive notifications carry hazard alerts (active wildfire near the commune, severe weather warning).

## Google Play Background Location Disclosure (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION)

- **Feature name:** `"Also follow where I am"` (`push.followTitle`, located in the Alerts sheet accessible from the bell button on the map, implemented in `src/components/nadhir/FollowLocation.tsx`).
- **Two-step opt-in and prominent disclosure checklist:**
  - [ ] Disabled by default; never requested at cold launch.
  - [ ] Step 1 (before foreground `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` prompt): shows `push.followTitle` (`"Also follow where I am"`) and `push.followBody`, explicitly stating `"following never sends your exact position"` and that only the commune alert subscription changes.
  - [ ] Step 2 (before background `ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION` prompt): displays the prominent in-app disclosure `push.followForeground` (_"Nadhir uses your location to keep your commune's alerts current, even when the app is closed or not in use. For now, following only works while Nadhir is open."_) directly above the explicit user action button `"Allow in the background"` (`push.followAllowBackground`), which invokes `CurrentCommune.requestBackground()`.
  - [ ] Foreground-only permission ("While using the app") remains a clean, supported mode without error loops.
- **Technical justification (on-device ADR-0006 resolution):**
  - Uses coarse, low-power updates (`PRIORITY_BALANCED_POWER_ACCURACY`, 15-minute interval, 5-minute minimum interval, 1 km minimum displacement) in `CommuneTracker.java`.
  - Resolves coordinates strictly on-device against bundled offline `assets/public/geo/communes.v1.json` (`CommuneResolver.java`).
  - Subscribes and unsubscribes Firebase topic `v1.commune.<code>.<lang>` directly via native Firebase SDK; zero coordinates and zero commune codes are sent to Nadhir servers.
- **ADR-0006 and ADR-0002 wording hygiene checklist:**
  - Always include the scoped phrasing `"following never sends your exact position"` (never make an unqualified claim across the whole app, because voluntary citizen reports and watch zones do transmit coordinates).
  - Follow `CONTEXT.md` and `docs/adr/0006-current-commune-is-resolved-on-the-device.md`: describe the feature as on-device commune following, never as continuous GPS surveillance, real-time position broadcasting, or proximity-radius queries.
  - Follow `docs/adr/0002` and `docs/adr/0003`: never use bare status words implying absence of danger, and never describe evacuation routing.
- **Play Console demo video requirement (owner-managed):**
  - Upload an unlisted demonstration video (`nadhir-background-location.mp4`) in Play Console -> App content -> Location permissions showing: opening the Alerts sheet, reading the prominent disclosure, toggling `"Also follow where I am"`, granting foreground location, tapping `"Allow in the background"`, and observing the resolved commune subscription update.
